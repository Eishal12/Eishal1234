# Organizing system analysis: Wikipedia

Wikipedia is a globally accessible, multi lingual digital encyclopedia design to gather and organize human knowledge. This document analyzes how Wikipedia structures its content, facilitates user interaction, and establishes relationships between resources. 

--- 
## 1. Primary Resources Being Organized
The core resources on Wikipedia are infromational articles written and maintained by a community of volunteer contributors.
* **Textual Articles:** Detailed encyclopedic entries covering topics spanning science, history, pop culture, and biography.
* **Multimedia Asssets:** Supporting media including images, historical photographs, audio samples, charts, and embedded video clips hosted on Wikimedia commons. 
* **Metadata and Reference:** Footnotes, external citations, revision histories, and talk  pages attached to every article to verify authenticy.
--- 
## 2. Primary Supported Interactions
Wikipedia's system is optimized for a wide spectrum of reader and editor activities.
* **Research and Fact checking:** Users search for specific facts using the central search bar on jump directly to verified source citations at the bottom of pages.
* **Exploratory browsing:** Readers follow internal links to jump from one concept to another organically.
* **Collaborative editing:** Authorized users can edit content, inspect talk pages for community discussion, and review previous versions of an article. 
--- 
## 3. Classification system
Wikipedia relies primarily on a hierarchical and category based classification system paired with faceted search features.
* **Category tree:** Articles are tagged at the bottom with broad and sub level categories like `Category:Modern Painters` $\rightarrow$ `Category:Visual Artists` 
* **Infoboxes:** Standardizes sidebar tables present structured metadata for rapid comparison across similar topics such as birthdates, locations, or key figures.
* **System Effectiveness:** This structure is highly effective for its broad audience, allowing novice readers to find quick summaries while providing researchers structured navigational pathways.
--- 
## Representation of Relationships
Relationships between individual resources are maintained through dynamic cross referencing.
* **Hyperlinks:** In text links directly connect related terms and concepts across different articles.
* **See also Section:** Manually curated lists at the end of articles direct users to relevant parallel topics. 
* **Navigation Templates:** Collapsible navboxes at the bottom of pages group related articles within a shared series or subfield.
--- 
## Conclusion
By combining explicitly hierarchical categories with flexible inline hyperlinking, Wikipedia creates an adaptable organizing system capable of hosting million of interconnected resources efficiently.
