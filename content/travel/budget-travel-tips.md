# Financial Tips for Budget Travel
Exploring the world without does not require a fortune. With clear financial strategies and flexibility you can stretch your travel fund and stay on the road longer. 

## Smart Savings Strategies
Finding affordables flights and lodging starts with timing and location flexibility

### Ways to Save
- Book flights mid week or during shoulder season.
- Cook simple meals using grocery stores instead of dining out every meal.
- Utilize public transit rather than taxis or uber.
- Take advantage of free museum days and walking tours.

> Travel is the only thing you can buy that makes you richer.

## Managing expenses on the Road
Tracking daily spending keeps you from overspending early in your trip.
