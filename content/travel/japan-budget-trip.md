# Japan Budget Travel Guide
Traveling to Japan does not have to break the bank. With strategic planning you can experience ancient temples, cities, and delicious cuisine while sticking to a reasonable budget. 

## Transportation and Accommodation
Public transit in Japan is world-class. Utilizing regional rail passes or prepaid IC cards keep transit simple and cost effective.

### Top Budget Accommodation
1. Capsule hotels for single travelers.
2. Traditional minshuku (family run guesthouses).
3. Budget business hotels near major train stations.

> Budget travel isn't about sacrificing quality; it's about spending intentionally on experiences that matter most.

## Food And Daily Expenses
Eating well on a budget is easy in Japan. Convenience stores like 7-Eleven offer fresh meals, while local ramen shops provide filling options.
