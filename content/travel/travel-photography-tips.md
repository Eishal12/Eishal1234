# Travel Photography Tips
Capturing stunning photos during your trips allows you to preserve memories and tell powerful stories. Good photography relies on understanding light, composition, and timing.

## Mastering Lighting and Composition
The best photo opportunities often happen during the "golden hour" the hour right after sunrise and right before sunset.

### Essential Composition Techniques
1. **Rule of Thirds**: Place points of interest along grid intersections.
2. **Leading Lines**: Use roads, pathways, or architecture to leas the viewers eye.
3. **Framing**: Use natural elements like arches or trees to frame your subjects.

> Composition is the art of seeing pictures, not just taking them.
