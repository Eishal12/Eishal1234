# DSLR vs. Mirrorless Cameras
When selecting digital camera equipment the primary choice is between DSLR and mirrorless camera syatems.

## Key Difference
| Feature          | DSLR                           | Mirrorless                     |
| :--------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| **Viewfinder**   | Optical (user internal mirror) | Electronic (Digital screen)    |
| **Body Size**    | Larger, heavier                | Compact, lighter               |
| **Battery Life** | Exceptional                    | Moderate                       |
| **Autofocus**    | Fast phase detection           | Advanced eye/ subject tracking |

## Sensor Compatibility
Both body types support full frame, APS'C, and micro four-third. They also accept both [[prime-vs-zoom-lenses]] using native or adapted mounts.
