# Macro Photography 
Macro Photography is extreme closeup photography, usually of very small subjects where the image size of the subject is life size or larger.

## Technical Requirements
* **Extremely shallow depth of field:** Requires precise and often narrow apertures like f/8-f/16.
* **Dedicated Macro lenses:** Fized [[prime-vs-zoom-lenses]] designed for 1:1 magnification
* **Stabilization:** Camera movement is magnified at macro distance making [[tripods-and-stabilization]] neccessary.
