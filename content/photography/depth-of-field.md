# Understanding Depth of Field
Depth of field is the zone of acceptable sharpness within a photo.

## What Controls DOF?
* **[[aperture]]:** Wide opening f/1.8 create shallow DoF small openings f/16 create deep DoF. 
* **[[focal-length]]:** Longer lenses produce a shallower DoF.

## Applications
* **Shallow DoF:** Blurs background to isolate subjects in [[portrait-photography]] and [[macro-photography]].
* **Deep DoF:** Keeps foreground and background sharp in [[landscape-photography]].
