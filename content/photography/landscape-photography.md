# Landscape Photography
Landscape photography aims to capture the vastness and beauty of natural environments.

## Strategies

1. **Maximize Sharpness:** Use a narrow [[aperture]] like f/8 or f/11 to achieve deep focus from foreground to background .
2.  **Use Stabilization:** Keep the camera still on [[tripods-and-stabilization]] during low light dawn or dusk shoots.
3. **Composition:** Utilize [[leading-lines]] and the [[rule-of-thirds]] to direct the viewers gaze across the frame. 

## Managing Light
Landscape photographers often rely on natural light during the Golden hour. In low light adjust your [[exposure-triangle]] settings specifically balancing low [[iso]] with a slower [[shutter-speed]].
