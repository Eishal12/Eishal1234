# Lightning Gear
Controlling light is essential for high quality photography when natural light is insufficient or harsh.

## Equipment
* **Speedlights and Strobe Lights:** Provide powerful bursts of light to illuminate dark scenes.
* **Soft boxes and Umberellas:** Diffuse harsh direct light into soft, flatttering illumination.
* **Reflectors:** Bounce ambient light back onto shadow areas.

Using artificial lightning allows photographers to maintain a lower [[iso]] setting and control the [[exposure-triangle]] during controlled studio shoots like [[portrait-photography]].
