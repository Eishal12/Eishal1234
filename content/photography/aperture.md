# Aperture
Aperture is the opening inside the lens that regulates light entry.

## Key concepts
* **F-Stops:** Measured as f numbers. e.g.. f/1.8, f/4, f/8, f/16. 
* **Inverse scale:** Smaller f numbers mean larger openings more light.

Aperture controls [[depth-of-field]]. Fast apertures on [[prime-vs-zoom-lenses]] excel in low light without needing extra [[lightning-gear]].
