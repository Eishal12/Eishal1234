# Tripods and Stabilization
Tripods and camera stabilization gear eliminate camera movement to produce razor sharp photos.

## When to use Stabilization
1. **Slow shutter speeds:** Long exposures in [[landscape-photography]] requires absolute stability.
2. **Macro shooting:** Microscopic focal shift in [[macro-photography]] demand stable platform positioning.
3. **Low light environment:** Allows shooting at lower [[iso]] levels without introduces blur from slow [[shutter-speed]] settings.
