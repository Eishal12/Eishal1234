# Color Theory
Color theory helps photographers select and combine colors within a frame to evoke specific and visual harmonies. 

## Key Color Schemes
* **Complementary Colors:** Colors opposite each other on the color wheel. e.g.. blue and orange create high contrast and vibrancy.
* **Analogous Colors:** Colors adjacent on the wheel. e.g.. red, orange, and yellow creates a cohesive feel.

## Application in Genres
In [[portrait-photography]] background color choices can make the subject pop. In [[landscape-photography]] natural golden hour tones create dramatic warm tones against cool shadows.
