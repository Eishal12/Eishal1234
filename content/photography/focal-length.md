# Understanding Focal length
Focal Length measured in mm determines the angle of view and magnification of your lens.

## Focal Length Categories
* **Wide angle 14mm-35mm:** Captures broad scenes. Excellent for [[landscape-photography]] .
* **Standard 50mm:** Mimics human visual perspective. popular in [[street-photography]] .
* **Telephoto 85mm-300mm:** Brings distant subjects close. Great for [[portrait-photography]].

Choose between fixed focal lengths or adjustable ranges in [[prime-vs-zoom-lenses]] .
