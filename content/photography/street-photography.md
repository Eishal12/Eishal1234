# Street Photography
Street photography records everyday life and candid moments in public spaces.

## Techniques and setup
* **Fast shutter speed:** Use a rapid [[shutter-speed]] 1/250s or faster to freeze motion.
* **Compact setup:** Small bodies from [[dslr-vs-mirrorless]] allows photographers to remain discreet.
* **Composition:** Look for dramatic shadows using [[color-theory]], intriguing architectural [[leading-lines]] or unique subject [[framing]] .
