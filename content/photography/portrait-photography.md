# Portrait Photography
Portrait photography focuses on capturing the personality, expression, and mood of a person or group of people.

## Essential Techniques
* **Focus on the Eye:** The subject's eye should always be the sharpest point in the image.
* **Control Backgrounds:** Use a wide [[aperture]] to a shallow [[depth-of-field]] blurring distracting background element.
* **Lightning Control:** Use sort of camera light or reflectors from your [[lightning-gear]] setup to avoid harsh shadows.

## Recommended Equipment

* **Lenses:** Mid telephoto [[prime-vs-zoom-lenses]] such as 85mm are ideal due to minimal facial distortion. 
* **Focal Length:** See [[focal-length]] for details on how different lens choices affect facial proportion.
