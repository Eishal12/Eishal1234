# Prime vs Zoom Lenses
Photographers chooses between prime lenses fixed focal length and zoom lenses variable focal length based on quality and convenience .

## Comparison
* **Prime Lenses:** Offers wider maximum apertures. e.g.. f/1.4, f/1.8 and sharper optical elements perfect for shallow [[depth-of-field]] in [[portrait-photography]]. 
* **Zoom Lenses:** Provide versatile [[focal-length]] ranges e.g.. 24-70mm ideal for fast paced [[street-photography]] or dynamic [[landscape-photography]] .
