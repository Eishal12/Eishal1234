# Negative Space
Negative space is the empty or uncluttered area surrounding the main subject of a photograph.

## Why Use Negative Space?
* Emphasizes the main subject by removing competing visual distractions.
* Evokes feelings of simplicity, isolation, or calm.

Combine negative space with the [[rule-of-thirds]] to create minimal, impactful compositions in [[street-photography]] or minimalist [[portrait-photography]].
