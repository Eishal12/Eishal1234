# The Rule of Thirds
The rule of third is a composition guidelines that proposes dividing an image into nine equal rectangles using two horizontaland two vertical gridlines.

## How to use it
* Place key subjects along the gridlines or at their intersection point .
* Position horizon along the upper or lower horizontal lines rather than in center.

## Application in Genres
**[[landscape-photography]]:** Align the horizontal on the top third line to emphasize the fore ground or on the bottom line to emphasize the sky.
**[[portrait-photography]]:** Align the subject's eyes along the top horizontal grid line for natural visual balance.

> **Tip:** Combine the Rule of Thirds with [[leading-lines]] or [[negative-space]] to make your composition even stronger.
