# Framing Techniques
Framing involves using elements in the foreground of a scene to create a natural border around the main subject.

## Benefit of Framing
* Adds context, depth, and scale to the photo.
* Directs the viewer's eye straight toward the primary focus point. 

## Examples
Common natural frames includes archways, tree branches, windows, and doorways. Using framing alongside [[leading-lines]] and the [[rule-of-thirds]] strengthens the overall composition in [[street-photography]]
