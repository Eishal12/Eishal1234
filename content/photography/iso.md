# Understanding ISO Sensitivity
ISO measures your camera sensor's sensitivity to light.

## Choosing the Right ISO
* **Low ISO 100-400:** Produces clean crisp images with high detail. Use in bright daylight.
* **High ISO 3200+:** Allows shooting in dark environments without flash but introduces digital noise grain.

Larger [[camera-sensors]] handle high ISO settings much better with reduced grain.
