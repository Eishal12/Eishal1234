# Camera Sensors
The image sensor is the electronic component inside a camera that captures light and converts it into a digital image .

## Sensor Sizes
* **Full Frame:** Matches the size of traditional 35mm film, providing optimal low light performance and dynamic range.
* **APS-C (Crop Sensor):** Smaller and lighter giving lenses a crop factor that increase apparent zoom.
* **Micro Four Thirds:** Very compact sensor design common in mirrorless travel setups.

## Impact on Shooting
Sensor size directly impacts [[iso]] performance and [[depth-of-field]]. Larger sensors capture cleaner images in low lights without excessive noise when choosing camera bodies in [[dslr-vs-mirrorless]].
