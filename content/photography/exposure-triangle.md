# The Exposure Triangle 
The exposure triangle shows how three settings work together to balance brightness in an image.

## The three Components

1. **[[aperture]]:** Controls lens opening size and [[depth-of-field]].
2. **[[shutter-speed]]:** Controls exposure duration and motion blur.
3. **[[iso]]:** Controls sensors light sensitivity.

Mastering exposure balance is crucial for all styles including [[portrait-photography]] and [[landscape-photography]].
