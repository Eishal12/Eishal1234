# Leading Lines
Leading lines are visual paths real or implied with in a photo that guide the viewer's eye toward the main point of interests. 

## Common Types of Lines
* **Roads and Paths:** Pull the viewer deeper into the scene.
* **Architectural Edges** Create strong structural pattern in urban shooting.
* **Fences and Shorelines** Guide focus horizontally or diagonally across the frame.

Combining leading lines with [[negative-space]] crates clean, delibrate visuals in both [[landscape-photography]] and [[street-photography]].
