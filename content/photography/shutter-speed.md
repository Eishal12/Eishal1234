# Shutter Speed
Shutter speed is the duration of time the camera shutter stays open, allowing light onto the sensor. It works alongside [[aperture]] and [[iso]] as part of [[exposure-triangle]].

## How Shutter Speed Affects Images
* **Fast Speed 1/1000s:** Freezes fast motion instantly. ideal for [[street-photography]].
* **Slow speed 1/2s or slower** Creates motion blur. Perfect for smoothing water in [[landscape-photography]].

> **Tip:** When using slow shutter speeds, use [[tripods-and-stabilization]] to prevent camera shake.
